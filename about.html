The 3 Pillars of Swaranthara

Foundations that define every lesson, practice session, and recital.

1. Rigorous Pedagogy

Proper posture, finger agility, ear-training, and staff notation from day one.

2. Joyful Community

Group ensemble jams, peer collaboration, and supportive family recitals.

3. Modern Technology

Yamaha synthesizer programming, DAW arrangement, and studio audio practice.
